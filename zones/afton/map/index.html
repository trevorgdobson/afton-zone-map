About this sheet

Frame: USGS Waynesboro East 7.5-minute quadrangle, 38°00′ to 38°07′30″ N, 78°45′ to 78°52′30″ W. The Zone is a circle of 2.5 km radius on Rockfish Gap (38.0315 N, 78.8578 W); it runs about 1 km off the west edge of the sheet. Grid squares are 250 m, lettered A to X west to east and numbered 1 to 24 south to north; the two digits after the dash are tenths of a square east and north. Bearings are degrees from true north. The built-in drawing is a sketch from memory and is only approximate.
