# Zone: Afton Mountain

The Zone is on Afton Mountain at Rockfish Gap, Virginia, where the Blue Ridge Parkway and Skyline Drive meet above Interstate 64 and US 250. The players know the area. Use it as it really is.

- **Centre:** Rockfish Gap, about 38.0315 N, 78.8578 W, the corner where grid squares L, M, 12 and 13 meet. The old Blue Ridge Tunnel runs under it.
- **Extent:** roughly 2.5 km around the gap. Fix the exact perimeter on the first run and never move it.
- **Town:** Waynesboro, at the western foot, is where stalkers live, drink and sell. The Institute is in Charlottesville, to the east.
- **Base map:** USGS Waynesboro East and West 7.5-minute quadrangles (1973). The field atlas has 36 numbered sheets of 4 by 4 squares, sheet 1 at the north-west, reading across, with contours in feet.
- **Lie of the land:** this mountain is not a simple west face and east face. The perimeter crosses several ridges, the gap itself is the low point at about 540 m, and much of the high ground to the south-west is a nearly level ridge top near 700 m with an abandoned golf course and houses on it. Check, never assume.
- **People and season:** small-town Appalachia. Winters bring frost and bare hardwoods; the ground is walkable all year.
- **Check:** `python terrain.py --zone afton at M12-55` should report about 542 m.
