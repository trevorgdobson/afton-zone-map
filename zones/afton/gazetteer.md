# Afton Mountain gazetteer

Real ground for the game master, in the atlas grid (250 m squares, A to X west to east, 1 to 24 south to north;
a reference such as M12-37 adds tenths east and north; the gap is the corner of L, M, 12 and 13).
This file holds no game secrets. It describes the real place as mapped today: in the fiction, anything inside the
perimeter has had decades of neglect. Use it so that slopes, roads, the railway, streams and buildings are where the
player's atlas shows them. It is not a list of what is in the Zone.

Sources: terrain from USGS 3DEP lidar; named places and lines from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
