# Seven Rila Lakes gazetteer

Real ground for the game master, in the atlas grid (250 m squares, A to X west to east, 1 to 24 south to north;
a reference such as M12-37 adds tenths east and north; the centre, the corner of L, M, 12 and 13, is the Lower Lake (Dolnoto ezero, Долното езеро), lowest of the Seven Rila Lakes; the centre point is in the middle of the lake).
This file holds no game secrets. It describes the real place as mapped today: in the fiction, anything inside the
perimeter has had decades of neglect. Use it so that slopes, lakes, trails, streams and buildings are where the
player's atlas shows them. It is not a list of what is in the Zone. All heights and distances are in metres.
Names are given in Latin letters (name:en where OpenStreetMap has one, else transliterated) with the local spelling in brackets,
which is what the player's atlas prints.

Sources: terrain from Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (30 m, 1 arc-second, a radar surface model), cubic-resampled to 10 m; named places, lines and land cover from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
The elevation model's true resolution is 30 m: slopes are averaged over that distance, so short steps, crags and gullies are smoothed away and real slopes are locally steeper than the table says.
