# How to play Zone Stalker

You are a stalker in the town beside a Zone: a piece of real mountain where something visited and left. You go in at night, find a way through things nobody understands, carry out what you can, and sell it. An AI is the game master. You do not need to have read *Roadside Picnic*, and the game will not spoil it.

A first night takes about an hour. Say you are new and it will keep things simple.

## What you need

- An AI chat that can run code. **Claude** works best (Pro or higher, with code execution switched on). ChatGPT with file uploads should also work.
- Optional: the field atlas (a PDF map you can mark up on a tablet or print) or the web map. You can play without either.

## Starting in Claude

Paste the starter message you were sent into a new chat. It tells Claude where to fetch the rules and maps. Then answer its questions.

If Claude says it cannot download anything, use the file method below.

## Starting in ChatGPT or another AI

1. Upload `zone-stalker-guest-pack.zip` to a new chat.
2. Send: "Unzip this, read START.md and do what it says."

## Things worth knowing before your first night

- **Nothing in the Zone is random.** Every strange thing behaves the same way every time. Watch, test, and write down what you learn. Your notes are worth more than your gear.
- **Throw a bolt before every step you have not already taken.** Ask the barman why.
- **Set a time to turn back, and keep it.**
- **You can ask how long anything will take.** Asking is free. Doing costs time.
- **You can die.** It will have been signalled first.
- **Keep the SAVE file** the game gives you at the end. To play again, start a new chat the same way and attach it.
- **Do not open `gm-only-do-not-open/`** in the pack. It holds how everything works, and finding that out is the game.

## Playing as a group

Everyone plays their own stalker in the same Zone, so what you learn is true for your friends too. Compare notes. Ask the game for "a report from the Zone" after a run and post it to the group. Nobody will tell you whether your friend's theory is right.
